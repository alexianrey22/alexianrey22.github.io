Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information PostDeck handles and how. In short: PostDeck runs on your own computer, keeps your data there, and only talks to TikTok to publish the videos you choose.

1. Information PostDeck handles

  • TikTok account information received when you connect an account: your open ID, display name, username and profile picture URL.
  • Access tokens issued by TikTok, which let PostDeck publish to your account on your behalf.
  • Your content: the videos you add to your library, and the captions, hashtags and schedules you set.

2. Where it is stored

All of this information is stored locally on your computer, in the PostDeck folder. PostDeck has no server of its own: the developer does not receive, collect or have access to your videos, account information or tokens.

3. How it is used

Your information is used only to show your connected accounts in the app and to upload and publish the videos you choose to the TikTok accounts you select, at the time you choose. It is not used for advertising, profiling or analytics.

4. Sharing

Your videos, captions and account tokens are sent only to TikTok, through TikTok's official API, when you publish. PostDeck does not sell, rent or share your information with anyone else. TikTok's handling of your data is governed by TikTok's Privacy Policy.

5. Retention and deletion

You stay in control of your data at all times:

  • Delete a video from your library in the app to remove it from your computer.
  • Disconnect an account in the app to delete its tokens and profile information.
  • Delete the PostDeck folder to remove all data completely.
  • You can also revoke PostDeck's access at any time in your TikTok settings, under Security and permissions, then Apps and services permissions.

6. Security

Data stays on your device and connections to TikTok use HTTPS. You are responsible for keeping your computer and the PostDeck folder secure.

7. Children

PostDeck is not intended for anyone under the minimum age required to use TikTok in their country.

8. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. The date at the top of this page shows the latest version.

9. Contact
